I am a first year medical (MD) student at Dalhousie University in Halifax, Canada, and a recent Engineering Science DPhil (PhD) graduate from the Institute of Biomedical Engineering at the University of Oxford, where I studied as a Rhodes Scholar. I pursued my DPhil under the supervision of Prof. Daniel Bulte within the Biomedical Image Analysis cluster. My research, at the intersection of engineering and medicine, focused on investigating the pupillary light response as a potential tool to measure cerebrovascular reactivity (CVR), an important metric of brain vascular health, to identify early indicators of Alzheimer’s disease and dementia. To do this, I designed and employed experimental methods to collect pupillometry, physiological, lifestyle, genetic, and transcranial Doppler ultrasound and MRI data from volunteers, to investigate the intersection of Alzheimer's disease risk factors, cerebrovascular reactivity, and pupillometry. I am particularly interested in incorporating an intersectional lens into health research, including assessing sex-specific factors and their relation to Alzheimer’s disease and dementia. I am a registered Engineer-in-Training with Engineers Nova Scotia, and I have collected over a year of engineering work experience in the fields of medical imaging and medical tech development, software development and testing, and in wired and wireless communications. I am always looking for new experiences, and to learn new things. 

I am a passionate advocate for equity, diversity, and inclusion – something I believe strengthens any discipline. With this interest for EDI and science outreach, I completed a Science Communication Training Fellowship with the Association for Research in Vision and Ophthalmology, was the Equality and Diversity Officer for the Keble College (Oxford) Graduate Student Society, and was an EDI Fellow for the Mathematical, Physical, and Life Sciences Division of the University of Oxford. At Oxford, I rowed for the Oxford University Boat Club in the Oxford-Cambridge Reserve Boat Races, and was President of the Keble College Boat Club. 

Aside from work and extra curricular involvements, I enjoy playing and listening to music, rowing, and am a major baseball fan (go Toronto Blue Jays!).
